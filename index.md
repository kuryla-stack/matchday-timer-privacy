# Matchday Timer Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-09-26

Matchday Timer is an offline match control board. This privacy policy explains the limited information handled by the app.

## Information stored on your device

The app stores match setup details, event timelines, saved results, onboarding status, and display preferences in local device preferences. This information is used only to provide the app features you request. The app does not require an account.

## Information sent elsewhere

The app does not send match records, device identifiers, contact details, or usage events to a server. The app has no network feature, advertising service, analytics service, crash reporting service, or account service.

## Sharing

Because the app does not collect or transmit personal information, it does not sell, rent, or share personal information with third parties.

## Retention and deletion

Local match records remain until you delete individual history entries, use Reset match data, or remove the app. Removing the app may also remove its local preferences according to the device platform behavior.

## Children

The app is a sports utility and does not knowingly collect information from children or any other person.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the owner will update the published policy text and effective date.
